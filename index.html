01THE PROMISE

        
Money without rulers.

        
Bitcoin is money with no issuer and no counterparty — a ledger kept by tens of thousands of independent computers and verified with pure mathematics. Nobody can print it, freeze it, or vote its rules away. Not a bank. Not a state. Not even Satoshi.
